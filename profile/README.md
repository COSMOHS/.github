# Welcome to COSMOHS GitHub
![logo](/COSMOHS%20logo.png)

COSMOHS is a nationwide Swedish cohort examining the health, comorbidity and health-related quality of life among people living with HIV. By linking the Swedish nationwide HIV quality register, InfCareHIV, to Sweden's national health and population registers, we can follow health outcomes for people with and without HIV across more than 40 years

In the interest of transparency, we have include codes from our research group's published research.
